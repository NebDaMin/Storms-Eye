&l Unique Equipment &r
- Falconry Glove: Allows for holding the bald eagle in hand.
- Falconry Hood: Allows for direct control of bald eagle. This can be removed with shears.

&l Combat Control notes &r
- When holding the Eagle with a falconry glove, but without the hood. Aiming at a mob and left clicking will cause the Eagle to attack the mob until it is dead and then will return to you.

&l Special Ability: Direct Control &r
- When holding a falconry glove and right clicking to pick up a bald eagle wearing a falconry hood. press left click to release the Bald Eagle and directly control it. Left Shift to end the effect and the eagle will return.
- If flying too far away from the player the eagle will attempt to return on its own. Be careful as it can get caught on obstacles.
- The Eagle will automatically attack entities it flies into.
