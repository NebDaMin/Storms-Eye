&l Special Abilities &r
- Storage Chest Space is extremely large.
- Leader Elephants can be fed Wheat while riding them to make them do a charge attack.
- A carpet can be placed on an elephant for decoration.
- Carpet and Chests can be removed with shears.

&l Notes on Variants and Taming &r
- Elephants exist in Leader (Long Tusks) and Standard Variants. Only Standard and Baby elephants can be tamed by the player. To obtain a tamed Leader elephant. you must tame it while it is a baby.
